Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy covers Neon Desk Pipeline, the tool The Neon Desk uses to publish its own videos.

Who uses it

Only the owner of The Neon Desk. The tool publishes and manages videos on the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and reads the analytics of those accounts. Nobody else signs in to it.

What data it touches

Through the YouTube API Services, the tool reads and writes data only for The Neon Desk's own channel: video uploads, titles, descriptions, captions and channel analytics. It does not collect personal data from viewers or anyone else.

What happens to that data

The data stays with the channel owner. We don't sell it, share it or use it for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the tool's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other terms

By using YouTube features through this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
